## What shipped

This covers 15 and 16 November. The digest on 16 November went out without a summary, so its work is included here.

### devantler.tech and the client portal

**New**

- Clients can sign in to the portal, by invitation only. ([#6389](https://github.com/devantler-tech/platform/pull/6389))
- Clients can sign in with GitHub, and the portal has its own sign-in service. ([#6313](https://github.com/devantler-tech/platform/pull/6313), [#6356](https://github.com/devantler-tech/platform/pull/6356), [#6379](https://github.com/devantler-tech/platform/pull/6379))
- The portal drafts proposals from a request and the published prices, and passes approved orders to the bookkeeping system. ([#585](https://github.com/devantler-tech/business-site/pull/585), [#587](https://github.com/devantler-tech/business-site/pull/587))
- Clients can pay by card or MobilePay, and every card payment gets full VAT paperwork from Stripe. ([#588](https://github.com/devantler-tech/business-site/pull/588), [#607](https://github.com/devantler-tech/business-site/pull/607))
- Clients can end a monthly plan or a single add-on themselves, and withdraw from an order within 14 days. ([#608](https://github.com/devantler-tech/business-site/pull/608), [#627](https://github.com/devantler-tech/business-site/pull/627), [#610](https://github.com/devantler-tech/business-site/pull/610))
- Clients get emailed order confirmations and withdrawal receipts. ([#615](https://github.com/devantler-tech/business-site/pull/615))
- The site publishes fixed prices, optional hosting, the launch offer and the standard add-ons, and clients can order the add-ons. ([#625](https://github.com/devantler-tech/business-site/pull/625), [#626](https://github.com/devantler-tech/business-site/pull/626), [#623](https://github.com/devantler-tech/business-site/pull/623))
- The site has a privacy notice and customer terms in English and Danish. ([#601](https://github.com/devantler-tech/business-site/pull/601))
- The new Devantler Tech logo is on the site, the platform's home page and the sign-in pages. ([#612](https://github.com/devantler-tech/business-site/pull/612), [#6361](https://github.com/devantler-tech/platform/pull/6361))

**Improved**

- Former clients' records are deleted automatically after three years. ([#616](https://github.com/devantler-tech/business-site/pull/616), [#620](https://github.com/devantler-tech/business-site/pull/620))
- The site now runs on our own platform instead of GitHub Pages, including the bare domain. ([#586](https://github.com/devantler-tech/business-site/pull/586), [cloudflare#78](https://github.com/devantler-tech/cloudflare/pull/78))
- A restore of the portal's database has been tested from backup. ([#6391](https://github.com/devantler-tech/platform/pull/6391))
