## What shipped

### Platform

**New**

- Company Email can now send to outside addresses. Production's mail server sends through Amazon's mail relay, with its own send-only login, and may reach nothing else on the internet. ([#6252](https://github.com/devantler-tech/platform/pull/6252), [#6236](https://github.com/devantler-tech/platform/pull/6236), [#6173](https://github.com/devantler-tech/platform/pull/6173))
- The platform is ready to serve devantler.tech, its www name and the customer portal for the business site, and the business site's staging copy is reachable again. ([#6188](https://github.com/devantler-tech/platform/pull/6188), [#6214](https://github.com/devantler-tech/platform/pull/6214))

**Improved**

- The secrets service can now move to a new release safely: its three servers are replaced one at a time, each only after health and quorum checks pass and production has applied the change. This unblocks an update that had waited over a month. ([#6213](https://github.com/devantler-tech/platform/pull/6213), [#6241](https://github.com/devantler-tech/platform/pull/6241))
- Why production changes took six to twelve hours to deploy is now measured and decided: the merge queue is fast, but its jobs wait for shared build machines. ([#6207](https://github.com/devantler-tech/platform/pull/6207))

### KSail

**Fixed**

- Saying no to `ksail cluster delete` now leaves the cluster exactly as it was. Before, it had already disconnected the cluster's image mirrors, so a later delete left them behind. ([#8359](https://github.com/devantler-tech/ksail/pull/8359))
- When a K3s cluster command fails, KSail now shows k3d's own reason, such as a port already in use, instead of always blaming Docker. ([#8358](https://github.com/devantler-tech/ksail/pull/8358))

**Improved**

- Every update of a Hetzner cluster now says whether a reserved floating address is left over and unused, not only updates that change the endpoint. ([#7838](https://github.com/devantler-tech/ksail/pull/7838))

### Agent Improver

- Agents can no longer tie up a session with a foreground loop that keeps asking GitHub for news; they wait with a background watcher instead. ([#5029](https://github.com/devantler-tech/monorepo/pull/5029))
- The digest check no longer mistakes numbers inside links for phone numbers, so a correct summary is not held back. ([#5033](https://github.com/devantler-tech/monorepo/pull/5033))
